# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 August 2026_

Memoryful is a personal journal. This page explains what we collect, who else handles it, and
what you can ask us to do about it.

Contact: **vlad0307b@gmail.com**

## What we collect

**Email address.** The only thing required to sign in; the service uses no passwords.

**Profile details, if you provide them.** Name, age, a short biography, country and city, and
a profile image.

Age is used to keep the assistant's suggestions appropriate to the person reading them — what
suits someone studying differs from someone in work, and some suggestions are not suitable for
a young person. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

**Journal content.** Day descriptions and entries, uploaded images, tags, and trackables such
as step counts.

**Assistant conversations.** The messages you send and the replies you receive.

We do not collect your date of birth, gender, phone number or contacts. The app contains no
advertising or analytics tracking.

## Journal content sent to AI providers

When you use insights, suggestions or the assistant, the relevant entries and messages are
sent to an AI provider to generate a response. Those providers are **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**
and **Google**.

Which one receives a request depends on the model selected, which you can change in
**Settings → AI**. Each provider handles what it receives under its own terms.

The journal works without these features. If you would rather your entries were not sent, do
not use them.

## Service providers

- **Neon** — the database holding your account, entries, tags and trackables
- **Google Cloud Storage** — uploaded images
- **Upstash** — caching and active sign-in records
- **Resend** — email delivery
- **OpenAI, Anthropic, Google** — insights, suggestions and assistant replies

Resend currently carries only sign-in codes, which contain no journal content. If we later
send summaries drawn from your entries, those emails would pass through Resend, and we will
update this page first.

## Where your data is stored

Your account and journal are stored in the European Union. The AI providers may process what
you send them outside the EU, including in the United States.

## Signing in with Google

Google provides your email address, whether it has verified that address, your name, and its
identifier for your account. We use the verified address to find or create your account.

We do not import your Google profile picture, and request no access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar
or Contacts. Google sign-in is optional; the email code method remains a full alternative.

## Sessions

Signing in stores a small file in your browser so you stay signed in between visits. It is not
used for advertising or for tracking you across other sites. Signed-in devices are listed
under **Settings → Account**, where you can sign out of any of them.

## Sharing and marketing

We do not sell your personal information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Some assistant features are expected to become paid. Payment will be handled by an established
payment provider, and we will describe that here before anyone is charged.

## Retention

We keep your account and its contents until you ask us to delete them. Entries are not removed
on a schedule.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- provide a copy of the information we hold about you
- correct anything inaccurate — most of it is editable in Settings
- delete your account and its contents
- stop using your information in a particular way

Email **vlad0307b@gmail.com**. If you are in the EU or UK and our response does not satisfy
you, you can complain to your national data protection authority.

Deletion is not yet self-service. Email us from the address on the account and we will remove
the account, its entries and its images.

## Age

Memoryful is for people aged 13 and over.

If you are in the EU or UK and under 16, a parent or guardian may need to agree to your use of
the service; the exact age depends on the country you are in.

If you believe someone under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

## Changes

If our handling of your information changes in a way that affects you, we will update this
page and the date above.
